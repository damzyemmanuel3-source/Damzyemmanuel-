About the university

Education that shapes more than a career.

Adeleke University is a private, faith-based university located in Ede, Osun State, South-West Nigeria. Established in 2011 by Dr. Adedeji Adeleke through the Springtime Development Foundation, the institution is guided by a commitment to teaching, research, innovation and service.

Its educational philosophy places importance on the development of the whole person: intellectually, socially, physically and spiritually. The university's motto captures this aspiration in three enduring words: Education, Excellence and Character.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions.

Where is Adeleke University located?

Adeleke University is located in Ede, Osun State, in South-West Nigeria.

When was Adeleke University established?

The university was established in 2011 by Dr. Adedeji Adeleke through the Springtime Development Foundation.

What is the university motto?

Education, Excellence and Character.

How can I apply for admission?

Use the official admissions portal linked on this page and read the university's current application guide. Requirements and available programmes can differ by session.

Where can I find tuition and other fees?

Fees can change by programme and academic session. Confirm the current approved fee schedule directly with the university before making any payment.

Are all programmes open for admission?

Not necessarily. Programme availability changes. The official website currently displays a notice that Medicine & Surgery and Law admissions are closed. Confirm the latest status before applying.

How can I contact the admissions office?

Use admissions@adelekeuniversity.edu.ng or the contact details published on the official university website.

Is this the official Adeleke University website?

No. This is a standalone website concept created as a downloadable HTML demo. It is not operated by or affiliated with Adeleke University. Official information and services are available through adelekeuniversity.edu.ng.
